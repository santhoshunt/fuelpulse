## What's new

- **Grades weigh cost as well as mileage.** Each fill-up gets one grade, Poor to Great: the lower of its km/L grade and its cost per km grade (price ÷ km/L). Great mileage from expensive fuel no longer looks like a win. Set both ranges for the active vehicle in Settings → Grading.
- **Tap a fill-up to see why.** A sheet slides up with where the fill-up sits in each range, the reason for its grade, and Open refill, Edit and Delete. Swipe to delete is gone, so nothing goes by accident.
- **What's in the tank now.** Enter today's odometer on the Dashboard (Update odometer reading) or from the Odometer widget, and the Dashboard estimates the fuel left and the range from it. Without a reading it shows the level right after your last refill, and says when that was. The reading also counts towards service km and reminders.
- **Home screen widgets.** Seven of them: Mileage, Quick add (Refill and Scan), Tank, Next service, Fuel spend, Best pump and Odometer. They update when you use the app.
- **Rank by km/L or by cost.** The pump and fuel grade comparisons on Analytics have a switch to rank by ₹ per km. The cheapest per km can be a different pump from the best km/L.
- **Your own logos and pictures.** Give any brand your own logo from its brand page, and choose a picture for each vehicle: Motorcycle or Scooter for a bike, or your own photo. Both go into backups.
- **Vehicle switcher.** The Dashboard shows the active vehicle's picture and name as a button at the top right. In Settings, tap a vehicle to edit it.
- **Fixes.** A service scheduled only by km now asks for the notification permission. A rescheduled service reminds again. Deleting data cancels its reminders straight away. In-app updates retry when GitHub's download server times out (the 504 error) before giving up.

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
