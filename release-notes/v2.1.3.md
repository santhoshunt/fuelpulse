## What's new

- **Odometer photos read the right number.** Odometer displays often show a 1 that looks like the letter I, and FuelPulse used to drop it: a TOTAL of 5198 came back as 98. Letters that look like digits now count as digits when they sit inside a number.
- **No more wrong guesses.** When you already have a fill-up for the vehicle, the odometer reading has to fall between your last reading and 5,000 km past it. If nothing on the photo fits, the field stays empty and FuelPulse says why, instead of filling in a speedometer number. A TRIP reading over 5,000 km is ignored the same way.
- **Better reports.** The scan report now shows the last odometer the reading was checked against.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.1.2, v2.1.1 and v2.0.0 with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
