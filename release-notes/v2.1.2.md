## What's new

- **Photo scan works now.** In v2.1.1, taking or picking a photo stopped at "Could not read the photo" before any reading started. The release build had stripped a part the text reader needs to start up, and this version keeps it.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.1.1 and v2.0.0 with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- If a scan reads a number wrong, tap Report under it and send the report. It includes the photo and every line FuelPulse saw.
