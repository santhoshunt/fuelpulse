## What's new

- **Readings page.** Tap the odometer on the Dashboard gauge, or All readings in Update odometer, to see every reading you've entered, with when and how: typed, from a photo, from the notification, or on a trip. Delete one and the reading before it becomes current. Saving a reading also shows Undo.

## Fixes

- Switching tabs is smoother, most of all in the first minute after the app opens. The animation no longer waits on the tabs reloading their data or on the home screen widgets.
- Mileage ranges and Cost per km ranges save each change as you make it, like the rest of Settings. Going back no longer loses them, and Reset offers Undo.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Nothing in the database or backups changes from v4.3.0. Your current odometer reading carries over as the first entry on the Readings page.
