## What's new

- **Pump photos fix their own misread digits.** A pump shows amount = litres × rate, rounded to the paisa. When the amount reads cleanly but litres or rate don't, FuelPulse tries the usual digital-display slips (a 7 read as 1, an 8 as 0, a thin 1 that vanishes) and keeps a fix only if the three numbers then match to the paisa. The form says how many digits it fixed so you can check them against the pump.
- **The rate fills even when the rest is unreadable.** Bright amount and litres windows often don't read at all. If the rate is the only number that came through, FuelPulse fills it, and the total follows as soon as you type the litres.
- **Premium fuel works.** Rates up to ₹200 a litre are accepted, so XP100, Speed 100 and Power 100 at ₹160 and more no longer fail the check.
- **Displays read as one line.** When the density and rate windows run together in a photo, FuelPulse now tries each part on its own.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.1.x and v2.0.0 with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Pump displays are still hard to read. Hold the phone square to the display, close enough that the numbers fill most of the photo, and avoid reflections. Send a report from any scan that goes wrong.
