## What's new

- **A new name and icon.** FuelPulse is now My H'ness, with a handlebar and open-road icon, because it keeps more than fuel. It installs over FuelPulse as the same app, with your data, widgets and Drive backups as they were.
- **Garage tab.** The Service tab is now Garage, with Service, Trips and Papers behind one switch.
- **Trips.** Start a trip from Garage → Trips. While it is on, a card on the Dashboard and a notification with Add fill-up, Odometer and End trip buttons show the km so far. End it at the reading where it really finished: pick the fill-up you made on the way home, or turn on "The trip ends here" when you save that fill-up. Each km of the trip is charged at the km/L of the full tank it was driven on. The fuel shows as Estimate until your next full fill-up measures it, then Measured, or Exact when the trip starts and ends on full fill-ups.
- **Papers.** Keep the RC, insurance, PUC certificate, licence and road tax with their numbers, dates and photos. Each one with an expiry date reminds you before it lapses: 1, 7, 15 or 30 days ahead, your choice.
- **Tyres and battery.** On each vehicle's page, note when a tyre or the battery was fitted and how long it lasts in km or months. A bar shows how much of its life is used, and a reminder comes near the end.
- **Bills and receipts.** Add photos or PDFs of the bill to a service, and of the receipt to a fill-up. They go into backups. A PDF opens in your PDF app.
- **Monthly fuel budget.** Set one per vehicle on its page. The Dashboard shows what is left this month and how much a day that allows, and the Fuel spend widget shows what is left.
- **Mileage drop alert.** When a full tank comes in 15% or more below your average, the Dashboard says so and suggests what to check. Change the limit, or turn it off, in Settings → Grading.
- **One period for Analytics.** A chip at the top sets the period for every card: the last 12 months, overall, or a custom range with quick picks such as this financial year. Ranges longer than a year draw a line you drag along to read any month, instead of bars too thin to read.
- **Price you paid.** A new Analytics card plots the price per litre of each fill-up and compares the average with the same months a year earlier.
- **Year report.** The share button on Analytics makes one image of a year: km, fuel and service spend, mileage and trips.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- The first launch adds tables for trips, papers, tyres and battery, and bills. Nothing you have is changed.
- Backup files made by v4.0.0 restore only in v4.0.0 or later, because they carry the new records and your bill photos. Backups from older versions still restore here.
- With many bill photos, Drive backups get bigger: each photo adds about 400 KB.
