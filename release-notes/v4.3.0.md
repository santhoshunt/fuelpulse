## What's new

- **Type the reading in the notification.** The weekly odometer reminder has an Enter km box. Type the reading there and it's saved without opening the app, with the widgets updated. A reading lower than your last fill-up comes back with the reason so you can type it again.
- **Checks you can undo.** A check shows a circle when it's due and a tick once done. Tap the circle when you've done it. Tap the tick to mark it done again early, or to undo a Done you tapped by mistake.
- **Your own gauge scale.** The Dashboard gauge still fits its scale to your fill-ups. Settings → Grading → Mileage ranges shows the range it uses now and lets you set your own From and To instead.
- **See the fill-up.** The mileage drop card's link now opens the Fuel list at that fill-up and tints it for a moment, instead of opening the fill-up's page.
- **Widgets you can tell apart.** Each home screen widget has its own name in the widget picker. The one-row widgets no longer cut off their bottom line, Next service is 4×2 when you add it, and the widgets update sooner after a change.

## Fixes

- The Next scheduled card shows km and days left in the same weight, in their own column.
- The Odometer button on the Dashboard trip card fits on one line.
- The check form labels its fields Days and Km, with a line saying what each means.
- The vehicle history PDF lists your checks.
- Coming back from the camera, the gallery or a file picker no longer asks you to unlock.
- Tall bill photos are saved smaller.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Nothing in the database or backups changes from v4.2.0.
- Widgets already on your home screen keep their size. Remove and add Next service again to get the 4×2 size.
