## What's new

- **A clearer fill-up page.** Fuel, Distance and Mileage are now separate sections. "Driven before this fill-up" and "Driven on this fill-up" replace the two look-alike distances, each with its date, and "Measured over" says which km the km/L came from.
- **Swipe up for the full fill-up.** On the Fuel list, swipe the fill-up sheet up to open its page, as well as tapping Open fill-up.
- **Undo after deleting.** Deleting a fill-up, service, trip, paper, tyre or battery, or check happens at once and offers Undo for a few seconds. Undo brings back its bills and photos too.
- **No more lost edits.** Going back from a form with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?" first.
- **Tyres and battery in Garage.** They now have their own card on Garage, Service, next to Checks.

## Changes

- One word for each thing across the app: "fill-up" everywhere, "litres", "distance since last fill-up" on the fill-up form, and "rating" for Poor to Great so it isn't confused with the fuel grade.
- Settings has a "Fill-up rating" section with both ranges and a new Dashboard gauge scale row, and an "Alerts and reminders" section.
- On Next scheduled, "Log this service" and "Change due date" replace Mark done and Reschedule. Change due date opens at the Next service part of the form.
- Analytics, Service: every card follows the chosen period, and the period named Overall is now All time.
- Analytics, Fuel: distance and litres sit with the spend in the top card.
- The Dashboard gauge always offers "Enter odometer reading" and "Readings", with or without a tank size.
- Fuel list rows show the price per litre in the same place on every row.
- Service, paper and tyre reminders open the page they are about. Papers "expire" instead of "renew".
- Danger zone, backup and restore texts say what they delete or keep. Error messages say what to do next.

## Fixes

- Delete all data now also clears odometer readings and gauge scales, and deleting a vehicle clears that vehicle's.
- Messages with a button, such as Undo, now close by themselves.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Nothing in the database or backups changes from v4.3.1.
- Widgets already on your home screen keep their size.
