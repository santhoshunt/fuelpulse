## What's new

- **Next check widget.** A new 4×1 home screen widget shows the check due soonest, such as "Tyre pressure, due in 2 days". Done marks it done without opening the app, and the app offers Undo the next time you open it.
- **A value with each check.** Done asks for an optional value, such as 32 psi or OK. The check's row shows the last one, and Edit check lists the earlier ones. Set a unit per check in Edit check.
- **Warranty for tyres and battery.** Each tyre and battery shows "Warranty until" with its date, in amber for the last 30 days. A reminder comes 30 days before the warranty ends.
- **Distance per week.** Analytics, Fuel has a bar for each week of the chosen period, worked out from fill-ups, odometer readings and trips, with the weekly average. Touch a bar for its dates and km.
- **City, highway or mixed.** On a full tank, the fill-up form asks where the tank was mostly driven. Analytics, Fuel compares the mileage of each once two have a tank.
- **Filters on the Fuel list.** Show one pump, one fuel grade, or one year or month. A line under the filters gives the count, spend, litres and average km/L of what's shown.

## Changes

- Backups now hold odometer readings, Dashboard gauge scales and the alerts and reminders settings. Replace restores them; Merge adds readings this phone doesn't have and keeps this phone's gauge scale. Restoring a backup made before 4.5.0 leaves them as they are.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- The database gains a unit and values on each check and the road tag on each fill-up. It upgrades by itself the first time 4.5.0 opens.
- A backup made by 4.5.0 still restores in older versions, which skip the readings, gauge scales, alert settings, check values and road tags.
- Add the Next check widget from your phone's widget picker; widgets already on your home screen keep their size.
