## What's new

- **Service records.** A new Service tab keeps each vehicle's services: date, odometer, workshop, the work done and what it cost. Schedule the next one by km, by months or both, and the tab shows what is due and how far off it is. The Dashboard shows the next service too.
- **Service reminders.** FuelPulse notifies you at 09:00 on the day a service is due, and when a fill-up brings the odometer within 100 km of a due reading. The first time you schedule a service, Android asks whether FuelPulse may send notifications.
- **Petrol pumps.** Save the pumps you use as a brand and a station, such as Bharat Petroleum, MG Road. Star the one you use most and the refill form picks it for you. Your existing fill-ups get one pump per brand automatically.
- **Fuel grades per brand.** Each brand now has its own grades: Speed and Speed 97 at Bharat Petroleum, XP95 and XP100 at Indian Oil, and so on. The form shows them as chips for the pump you picked. Your old fuel types stay on every brand, so nothing you logged loses its grade.
- **Pump comparison.** Analytics ranks your pumps by km/L. A Fuel and Service switch adds service spend by month and by type, your running cost per km with servicing included, and what is coming up.
- **Your own mileage ranges.** Each vehicle can set where Poor, Low, Fair, Good and Great start. Bikes and cars begin with their own defaults.
- **Vehicle details.** A vehicle now has a make and model, and editing it opens a full page.
- **Services from a spreadsheet.** Import and export has a service CSV template and an import that creates any missing workshop or service type.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- The first launch upgrades the database and keeps a copy of the old one beside it, in case anything goes wrong.
- Backup files made by v3.0.0 restore only in v3.0.0 or later. Backups from older versions still restore here.
