## What's new

- **A new look, with tabs.** FuelPulse now opens on a Dashboard, with Fuel, Analytics and Settings tabs along the bottom.
- **The Dashboard.** A speedometer-style gauge points at your average km/L, with the odometer on a seven-segment display inside the dial. Below it: fuel left after the last refill and the range it gives, your worst, last and best km/L, cost per km, this month's spend, the monthly average, the latest price and the last 12 tanks' mileage.
- **Light and dark, in 15 colours.** Choose light, dark or follow the phone in Settings → Appearance, and pick one of 15 palettes. Each pairs a main colour with a partner used for spend bars and the tank level.
- **Brand logos.** Shell, HP, Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, Nayara Energy and Jio-BP show their own logos everywhere a brand appears.
- **Fuel list and refill page.** Fill-ups are grouped by month, brand first, with km/L coloured from Poor (red) to Great (green). Tap one for the distance it covered, its cost per km and the tank level after it. Swipe left to delete.
- **Analytics.** Monthly fuel spend, mileage over time, brand and fuel grade comparisons and totals. Each chart has its own 1, 3, 6 or 12 month period.
- **Fill-ups have a date.** Set the date and time when you add or edit a fill-up, so one logged a day late lands in the right place.
- **Photo scan has its own screen.** Tap the camera on the Fuel tab, or "Scan photos to fill" on the form. Add the odometer and pump photos, check what was read, then fill the form. A gallery photo also sets the date. When the pump numbers don't agree, the form works out the litres from the amount and rate and offers them with one tap.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Your old accent colour doesn't carry over. FuelPulse starts on the Blue palette and follows your phone's light or dark setting until you change it in Settings → Appearance.
- Backup files from this version still restore in v2.0.0 and later. Older versions ignore the palette and theme setting.
