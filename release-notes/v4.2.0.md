## What's new

- **App lock.** Turn it on in Settings → Security and the app asks for your fingerprint, face or phone PIN when it opens. Choose when it locks again after you leave: immediately, after 1 minute or after 5 minutes.
- **Vehicle history as a PDF.** Import and export → Vehicle history (PDF) makes one file with the active vehicle's summary, every fill-up, service, tyre and battery, paper and trip, and each bill photo on its own page at the end. Handy when you sell the bike or claim insurance.
- **Checks.** Garage → Service has a Checks card for small jobs that repeat, such as tyre pressure every 14 days or chain lube every 500 km. Tap Done when you've done one. You get a notification when one falls due.
- **Odometer reading reminder.** A weekly notification asks for a reading, Sundays at 19:00 unless you pick another day and time in Settings → Reminders. It has Update and Photo buttons, and it skips the week if you entered a reading or a fill-up in the three days before.
- **Fill up full nudge.** After three partial fill-ups in a row the Dashboard reminds you to fill the tank once, since mileage can only be measured between full tanks.
- **Share a trip.** The share button on a trip makes an image card with the trip's route line, km, fuel, mileage and cost per km.
- **Trips to CSV.** Import and export → Trips (CSV) exports every vehicle's trips.

## Fixes

- Switching tabs now slides the way you moved, left or right, and fades between the two screens.
- Swipe a message at the bottom of the screen left or right to dismiss it.
- The odometer camera button lets you choose a photo from the gallery as well.
- Tapping outside a chart clears the point you touched.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- The database gains a table for checks. Backups now carry your checks. Restoring a v4.2.0 backup in an older version loads everything except the checks.
