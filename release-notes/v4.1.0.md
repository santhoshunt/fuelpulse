## What's new

- **Price from the pump.** Pick a saved pump on Add fill-up and the price per litre from your last fill-up there is filled in, with the date it came from. Type over it if the rate moved; a price you type or scan is never replaced.
- **Low fuel warning.** When an odometer reading leaves less than 50 km of fuel, you get a notification, once per refill. Change the distance, or turn it off, in Settings → Grading. The app knows what's left only when you enter a reading.
- **Trip widget.** A new home screen widget shows the trip on now with its km and an End button, or your last trip with Start.
- **Odometer from a photo.** The Update odometer dialog has a camera button that reads the odometer for you. The Odometer widget has a Photo button that opens the camera straight away.
- **Touch a chart to read it.** Drag along any line chart and a bubble shows the date and value at that point. On bar charts the bar you touch stays bright with its month and amount.
- **Smoother switching.** Tabs fade in as you switch. Fuel and Service on Analytics, and km/L and ₹ per km on the comparisons, fade through with a slight drift, and every switch's highlight slides across.
- **Garage.** Swipe between Service, Trips and Papers. The + button now sits next to the Garage title and adds to the list you're on.
- **Notification icon.** Notifications show a motorcycle in the status bar instead of a white square.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 and later with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Nothing in the database or backups changes from v4.0.0.
