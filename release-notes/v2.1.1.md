## What's new

- **Fill a fill-up from photos.** At the top of Add Fill-up, tap Odometer or Pump display, then take a photo or pick one from your gallery. FuelPulse reads the numbers on your phone, and the photo never leaves it.
- **Odometer photos.** A TOTAL reading fills the odometer. A TRIP A, TRIP B, TRIP 1 or TRIP 2 reading fills KM Travelled instead, since a trip meter counts from your last fill-up. The clock and the gear letter on the display are ignored.
- **Pump photos.** The first line on the display is the amount, the second is the litres, and the small box is the rate. FuelPulse fills litres, price and total only when litres × rate matches the amount, so one misread digit can't end up in your history.
- **You stay in charge.** Filled fields say "From photo" until you edit them, and nothing saves until you tap Save.
- **Report a misread.** Each scan has a Report button. It shares the numbers FuelPulse saw, with the photo, through any app you pick.

## Before you update

- This update installs over v2.0.0 with your data intact. The in-app updater offers it, or download `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
- Photo scan is new. Digital displays can fool it, so check the filled numbers before saving, and send a report when one comes out wrong.
